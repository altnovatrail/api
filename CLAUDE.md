# CLAUDE.md — working rules for this repository

If `spec/RULES.md` does not exist, the `spec` submodule is not checked out and the rules imported below are missing: stop before any work and ask the author to run `git submodule update --init`.

## Your role

You are the architect of `api`, a component of NovaTrail: the platform's only service in the first slice, which checks the account owner's token from the sign-in service, keeps the learners, their consent and attempts, keeps the learner model, which lives in its database as an append-only log of evidence, and runs the check, the hint ladder and the engine's choice with tutor, module-kit, learner-model and engine as libraries in its process. It calls AI providers only through tutor, stores neither the picture or sound sent for checking nor an own key, and writes no direct identifiers, IP addresses or content to its logs. Its documents under `docs/`, but for the epic archives `docs/plan/`, are written for the session of Claude Code that builds the component: write them so that it can act on them without asking what you meant. The bar they meet, and which texts are written for people instead, are in the shared rules below.

## The component

app calls api; api calls the sign-in service and its own database, and AI providers only through tutor, whose request types have no fields for the learner's or the account's identifiers, so the AI sees the work but not whose it is. Among the platform's parts, its database is the only server-side store of learners' data. It holds the account, learners' profiles, consent, attempts and observations, and the learner model, while strokes and photos from paper stay on the device and artifacts reach the server only if the account owner chooses so; the learner model lives there as an append-only log of evidence, whose skill state is a cache. The picture or sound sent for checking and an own key live only in the request's memory, and its logs carry no direct identifiers, IP addresses, work, readings or hint texts. It is built with tutor, module-kit, learner-model and engine at exact versions, takes spec as a Go module at the submodule's tag, with the module format's types, and the code of the API contracts as spec's nested module `contracts` at the same tag, and holds the module packages in its image, pinned in its configuration by a release of modules and its index, serving the files of their device part — media, LICENSE and NOTICE. It belongs to stage 2, with tutor, canvas, app, skill-map and infra, and with app it makes the first app, which the author alone runs locally. Before the first child come verifiable parental consent and the contracts with AI providers, П-1 and П-3. spec's cross-repository plan, COORDINATION.md, tracks that order.

Its card in spec is the one in `spec/docs/architecture.md` whose heading names `api`. The card is the summary and this repository's [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) is the detail: it lists what the card left to decide and describes the service; epic E1 decided what the card left, and its archive is [docs/plan/E1.md](docs/plan/E1.md).

## Documents

The documents under `docs/` are in English, but for the epic archives `docs/plan/`, which stay in Russian; RUN.md and `run/` are in English, but for the bodies of tasks. Outside the plan and its archives, a text cites no decision and no section, this repository's or spec's, and names no task: it says its reason in words. Only a claim about the outside world cites its one record, `sources 1.1`, or spec's, `sources 2.3 spec`.

- [docs/README.md](docs/README.md) — the list of the documents, the releases and how a change comes in, Contributing; README.md says only what `api` is and where it stands in the platform, with no command.
- [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md) — the component: what it does and does not do, its data and links, what is still to decide, and how it is built once that is decided. Its last section is a pointer: it names the record of `docs/sources.md` or `docs/measurements.md` where each reading it held went, for the decision log and the archives, which cite it.
- [docs/build.md](docs/build.md) — how `api` is built, checked, released, configured and run: the recipes, the variables, the commands and the jobs of a rollout, the sign-in in production, the run on the dev projects and with app.
- [docs/sources.md](docs/sources.md) — the records of external facts, by the parts of the service that `docs/architecture.md` describes, each with its source, its date and its quote; a claim cites its one record by its number: `sources 7.1`.
- [docs/measurements.md](docs/measurements.md) — the results of measurements: what was measured, how, on what, and the numbers, cited as `measurements 2.1`; architecture keeps only what follows from them.
- [docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md) — this repository's decision log: read it before proposing a change.
- [RUN.md](RUN.md) — the work plan, in epics: its rules, in its section Rules, and table 1 with the current epic's tasks, run one at a time, or between epics the tasks outside an epic. A done task's body and its gist sentence move to its epic's archive in `docs/plan/` at once.
- `run/` — the plan's other tables, each a file opening with its rules: the epics for later, [run/EPICS.md](run/EPICS.md); the questions to the author, [run/QUESTIONS.md](run/QUESTIONS.md); the technical debt, [run/DEBT.md](run/DEBT.md); the open tasks outside an epic, [run/TASKS.md](run/TASKS.md), of which a task reads only its own body. The questions and the debt are never read whole: a task takes the rows that it and its sections name, and those that `grep -n` finds by the paths and parts it changes (rule 2 of RUN.md).
- `spec/` — spec at `v0.19.14`, a git submodule and read-only here: the platform's vocabulary, formats, contracts, documents and decisions, and the rules imported below. Its documents are under `spec/docs/`. spec's own CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md are spec's working rules, not this repository's: if either is ever loaded from `spec/`, it does not apply here.

## Environment

Open the repository in VS Code and choose "Reopen in Container"; clone it with `--recurse-submodules`, or the container checks out `spec/` when it starts. Keep the clone in a folder named `api`, as `git clone` names it: `.claude/settings.json` keeps spec's own CLAUDE.md and its rules, `spec/.claude/rules/`, out of this repository's sessions by that path. It also compacts a session at 400 thousand tokens, fetches no claude.ai connector, runs spec's hook `UserPromptSubmit`, `spec/tools/session/`, which stops a prompt that starts a task other than the one the session has led and names `/clear` — one task is one session. The devcontainer loads the plugin `gopls-lsp` of spec's `claude-plugins/` by `CLAUDE_CODE_PLUGIN_DIRS`, which gives the session gopls's diagnostics after each edit of Go, once the folder is trusted. The container is spec's: Node.js, Go, buf and just, and the GitHub CLI and Claude Code on top. The tools of this repository's own code come with that code, at exact versions; how its container then differs from spec's is recorded as a decision. `just -f spec/justfile docs-check-repo` checks its documents: that its links lead somewhere, that the rule of citations holds — a record it cites of spec exists in spec/ at its tag —, that the decision log runs without gaps, and the tables of its RUN.md, and that its files, rules, decision entries, sections and rows keep within their budgets of size, by [docs-budgets.json](docs-budgets.json). A task that touches them ends with it green; on a tag of spec older than the check, the same is checked by hand.

**The checks of each part** are its rule, `.claude/rules/<part>.md`, with the part's files in its `paths`: Claude Code loads a rule only when the session reads or edits a file it matches, so before changing a part, read its rule; CI runs every check on every pull request (measurements 1.5 spec). The rules and their parts: [go](.claude/rules/go.md) — the Go code, its tests and the Go module; [modules](.claude/rules/modules.md) — `modules.json`, the reading of the packages and `cmd/modules-check`; [ci](.claude/rules/ci.md) — CI, the release and the devcontainer: `.github/`, `.devcontainer/`, the justfile, the `Dockerfile` and `scripts/`.
